Global Travel Briefing: India Digitalizes Airport Departures, LRS Travel Spend Rises, and US Visa Rules Tighten

NIVANTA NEWS DESK • AUGUST 27, 2026 • 02:41 PM

Indian outbound international travel is witnessing pivotal regulatory upgrades alongside sustained financial growth. Outward remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) surged by 20% year-on-year to $2.5 billion in June, largely driven by resilient overseas holiday demand and international investments.

In a major boost for passenger convenience, immigration authorities in India will discontinue physical boarding pass stamping for departing international travelers starting September 1, enabling a smoother transition to digital e-boarding passes. Concurrently, travelers heading to North America should note tightening visa enforcement, as US officials prepare a massive revocation targeting specific tourist and business visas linked to asylum claims.

  • Paperless Departures: Physical boarding pass stamping at Indian international departures ends September 1, simplifying immigration workflows.
  • Rising Travel Outflows: LRS remittances jumped to $2.5 billion in June, confirming strong spending on international luxury travel and overseas investments.
  • US Visa Scrutiny: Up to 200,000 US tourist and business visas face potential revocation under a targeted federal enforcement drive.
