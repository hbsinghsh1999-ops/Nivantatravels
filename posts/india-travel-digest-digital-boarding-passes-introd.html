India Travel Digest: Digital Boarding Passes Introduced and Outward Travel Remittances Surge

NIVANTA NEWS DESK • AUGUST 27, 2026 • 11:50 PM IST

Outbound travel from India is undergoing a rapid modernization alongside an unprecedented surge in luxury holiday spending. Recent Reserve Bank of India (RBI) data shows that outward remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) surged by 20% to $2.5 billion in June, heavily propelled by international travel and overseas leisure expenditures. This surge underscores the growing appetite among Indian travelers for high-end international destinations such as Thailand and the Maldives.

To support this growing volume of international flyers, Indian aviation and immigration authorities have announced a major policy update to streamline airport departures. Effective September 1, immigration officials will no longer mandate physical stamps on boarding passes for outbound international passengers, enabling a fully digital e-boarding pass experience at major Indian airports.

  • Seamless Indian Departures: Physical stamping of boarding passes by immigration authorities ends on September 1, significantly reducing processing times for international flights.
  • Surge in Travel Spending: LRS remittances for travel and overseas investments jumped significantly, pointing to robust demand for outbound leisure trips among Indian holidaymakers.
  • Evolving Visa Scrutiny: As global travel rules and immigration policies tighten across western nations, staying informed on official travel guidelines is more vital than ever.
