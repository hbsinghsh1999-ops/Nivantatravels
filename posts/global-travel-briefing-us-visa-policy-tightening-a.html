Global Travel Briefing: US Visa Policy Tightening and the Surge in Indian Destination Events

NIVANTA NEWS DESK • AUGUST 28, 2026 • 12:41 AM IST

In a major international travel policy shift, United States officials are set to initiate one of the largest mass visa revocations in history, potentially impacting up to 200,000 tourist and business visas. Travelers using non-immigrant visas are urged to maintain strict compliance with visa conditions and stay informed on immigration guidelines to avoid unexpected travel interruptions.

Concurrently, India's travel and event landscape is seeing rapid expansion, with the Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions (MICE) sector projected to grow by 12-14% annually over the next three to five years. Driven by rising disposable income and a demand for bespoke experiences, destination weddings are also skyrocketing with a 15-20% year-on-year growth rate, increasing demand for luxury hospitality both domestically and across premium island destinations like the Maldives and Thailand.

  • US Visa Scrutiny: Travelers holding US tourist (B1/B2) visas should ensure full documentation compliance amid impending revocation measures targeting asylum-linked filings.
  • MICE Sector Expansion: Corporate incentive travel and international conferences originating from India are set for steady double-digit annual growth.
  • Luxury Wedding Trends: Destination weddings continue to surge by up to 20% year-on-year, driving early bookings for exclusive resorts and private island venues.
