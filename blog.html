Thailand-India Connectivity Soars: IndiGo & Vietjet Thailand Expand Flight Routes

Air travel between India and Thailand is set for significant expansion, opening up new access points for both budget and leisure travelers across Indian cities. IndiGo has announced non-stop flights connecting Gaya to Bangkok starting October 26, enhancing direct international connectivity from tier-two hubs. Simultaneously, Vietjet Thailand is restarting its India–Thailand route network, offering competitive promotional fares starting from as low as INR 1.

These new flight options support Thailand’s broader strategy to drive inbound tourism from India by targeting high-growth sectors, including destination weddings, MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions), and experience-led travel. Increased route availability provides strategic flexibility for budget-conscious tourists seeking low-cost entry fares as well as luxury event planners organizing grand events abroad.

  • New Route Launch: IndiGo begins direct Gaya-Bangkok non-stop service starting October 26.
  • Airline Restart: Vietjet Thailand resumes India–Thailand operations with promotional fares from INR 1.
  • Strategic Focus: Enhanced flight options aim to boost Indian MICE, destination weddings, and experiential tourism.
